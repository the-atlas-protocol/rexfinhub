Methodology March 2026
Asia AUM
Collected monthly from brokerage firms and custodians across Asian markets
Some brokers report AUM directly in local currency (KRW, JPY), converted to USD at month-end FX rates. Others report share counts, priced using Bloomberg month-end closes
Futu HK: structurally unreachable — the broker does not release asset data without a commercial agreement. Positions are held at last-known shares and repriced at each month's Bloomberg
close. Flows for this bucket are mechanically zero; all MoM change reflects market performance
Oriental Harbour, ViewTrade, MooMoo (Singapore, Malaysia): quarterly reporters. Between reports, positions are held at last-known shares and repriced at Bloomberg close. Values are
replaced with actuals when the next report arrives. Oriental Harbour's data is sourced from Q1 13F filings (due mid-quarter following quarter-end)
Asset Plus Thailand: sold DRNZ — position has been zeroed as of March 2026
Global AUM
Sourced from Bloomberg as of each month-end
Covers all REX ETPs across ETFs and ETNs
Used to calculate each fund's percentage of global AUM held in Asia and to decompose AUM changes into market impact and investor flows
Estimated Flows
Each fund's month-over-month Asia AUM change is split into two components: market impact and estimated investor flows
Market impact is the portion of the change attributable to the fund's global price movement
Estimated flows are the residual — the AUM change not explained by price movement, representing net new investment or redemptions
These are estimates. Actual fund flows, where disclosed by managers, may differ
