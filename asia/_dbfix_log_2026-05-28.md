## Flow/market diligence -> found + fixed a repricing bug
- 6 funds reverse/forward-split between Mar and Apr (SMUP 29x, EOSU 40x, SNDU, GLXU, BERZ, CORD).
- `shares_invariant_reprice` held PRE-split shares x POST-split price = ~29x inflation on held
  positions. SMUP Asia was overstated $35.9M -> corrected to $11.7M. Fix: reprice all held
  positions by fund-total-AUM growth (split-immune) + carry prior shares (so held positions
  correctly contribute ZERO flows). April total $1,421M -> $1,397.92M.
- The REPORT's flow method (enrich: market_move = asia_prior x global_AUM_ratio; flows = residual)
  is itself split-immune, so per-fund flows are sane (SMUP +$5.4M, not -$82M).
