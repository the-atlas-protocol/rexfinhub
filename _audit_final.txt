    | Methodology March 2026
    | Asia AUM
    | Collected monthly from brokerage firms and custodians across Asian markets
    | Vendors report in various formats: USD totals, local currency (converted at month-end FX), share counts (priced via Bloomberg month-end closes), or aggregate amounts
    | Monthly reporters: values used as delivered
    | Quarterly reporters: between reports, positions are held at last-known shares and repriced at current-month Bloomberg closes. Flows for these positions are mechanically zero; all MoM
    | change reflects market performance. Values are replaced with actuals when the next report arrives
    | Aggregate-only reporters: where a vendor provides a total but not a per-fund breakdown, last-known per-fund shares are scaled proportionally to match the vendor's reported aggregate
    | Closed positions: when a vendor has fully liquidated its REX holdings, no positions are reported
    | Global AUM
    | Sourced from Bloomberg as of each month-end
    | Covers all REX ETPs across ETFs and ETNs
    | Used to calculate each fund's percentage of global AUM held in Asia and to decompose AUM changes into market impact and investor flows
    | Estimated Flows
    | Each fund's month-over-month Asia AUM change is split into two components: market impact and estimated investor flows
    | Market impact is the portion of the change attributable to the fund's global price movement
    | Estimated flows are the residual — the AUM change not explained by price movement, representing net new investment or redemptions
    | These are estimates. Actual fund flows, where disclosed by managers, may differ
    | Source: Bloomberg, broker reports, 13F filings. *Flow estimates derived from shares outstanding and NAV data; actual fund flows may differ. REX Financial · Page 9
